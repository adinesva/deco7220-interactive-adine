Audio Features

🔊 Loudness
The overall amplitude of a track, measured in decibels (dB).
Higher loudness values indicate louder tracks, often associated with more energetic or compressed production styles.
😊 Valence
A measure of musical positivity.
Tracks with high valence sound happy, cheerful, or uplifting, while low valence tracks feel sad, moody, or tense.
⚡ Energy
A measure of intensity and activity in a track.
Higher energy tracks feel fast, loud, and dynamic, while lower energy tracks are calmer and more relaxed.
🎙 Speechiness
The presence of spoken words in a track.
Higher values indicate more spoken content, while lower values represent purely musical tracks.
🕺 Danceability
A measure of how suitable a track is for dancing.
Tracks with high danceability have a clear rhythm, steady beat, and tempo that make them easy to move to.
04Top 10 Songs

Ranked by Spotify popularity score (0–100) from the dataset.
